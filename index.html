01 / THE STORY

From our home in Kannur to your home.

Farm Fresh Honey began in Kannur, Kerala, in 1990 with a simple idea: keep good honey close to its source.

What started as a home-based tradition has remained rooted in the same place. Our honey is sourced locally around our home, collected from honey bees in the local area and processed here with care.

We believe good food does not need to be complicated. It needs a genuine source, thoughtful handling and a taste worth coming back to.

06.02

10 facts about honey bees

01

Honey bee wings can stroke about 11,400 times per minute.

02

A queen bee can lay up to 1,500 eggs per day.

03

From guarding the hive to making honey, the colony relies on female worker bees to thrive.

04

A queen bee’s life expectancy is around 3–5 years.

05

Nectar is high in natural sugars, giving bees energy for their work.

06

Bees “dance” to let each other know where to find nectar, pollen and water.

07

Male bees, or drones, have one main job: mate with a queen.

08

Honey bees never sleep.

09

Bees get their food and nutrients from flowers.

10

One bee may make only about ½ teaspoon of honey in its entire life.
