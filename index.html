Understanding honey

01

Honey is a naturally sweet food made by honey bees.

02

Honey contains naturally occurring sugars and provides energy.

03

Honey colour and flavour may vary depending on the floral source.

04

Store honey according to the product's recommended storage instructions.

06.02

Facts about honey bees

01

Honey bees live and work together in organised colonies.

02

Worker bees collect nectar and pollen for the colony.

03

Honey bees communicate using movement, scent and other signals.

04

The queen bee is the main reproductive female in a colony.

05

Nectar provides bees with sugars that supply energy.

06

Bees help pollinate many flowering plants.

07

Male honey bees are called drones.

08

Worker bees perform different tasks as they age.

09

Honey bees use flowers as an important source of food.

10
